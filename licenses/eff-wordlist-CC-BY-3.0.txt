EFF Large Wordlist for Passphrases

slot.advance generates the initial password for Syncthing's web interface from five words
of the EFF Large Wordlist (7,776 words), included unmodified in the slot.advance program.

Wordlist: "EFF's Long Wordlist" by the Electronic Frontier Foundation (Joseph Bonneau, 2016),
https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2016/07/new-wordlists-random-passphrases
https://www.eff.org/files/2016/07/18/eff_large_wordlist.txt
