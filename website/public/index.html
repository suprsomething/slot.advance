Real sleep, lower standby drain

Close the lid and your RG SP genuinely sleeps, with your game held in memory. Open it and carry on straight away, with nothing reloaded.

In two separate 8-hour tests on a 0.5.0 test build, it used about 2% of the battery an hour, with Wi-Fi off or on.

Each block is 1% an hour on the battery gauge. ~7%: a modified 0.3.0 test build held in software standby (ordinary 0.3.0 shuts down after 3 minutes). ~2 to 3.2%: three extended sleeps on 0.4.0-beta.3, 0.4.0 and 0.4.1-beta.1. 0.5.0: the same reading with Wi-Fi off and on doesn't show identical standby use. Observed readings, not guaranteed battery life or one controlled comparison.

How it was measured

Sleep on your schedule

Choose from 3 minutes to 8 hours. After that it shuts down cleanly and restores your game at the next power-on.

Wi-Fi when you want it

Off by default. Switch it on in Settings and restart, join with the on-screen keyboard, and it reconnects by itself after you open the lid.

Your saves, in sync

Built-in Syncthing keeps your Saves and States in step with a computer or home server on your own network, while the handheld is awake.

Settings without leaving your game

Tap MENU for Resume, States, Settings and Exit Game. Shader, colour correction, fast-forward speed and sound are remembered for each game.

Your games, within reach

All, Recents and Favourites, and straight back to the last cartridge you played.

Save states, simplified

Five per game, browsed visually, with a check before anything is deleted.

Looks the part

Cartridge artwork, optional game titles and native 3× GBA scaling.
